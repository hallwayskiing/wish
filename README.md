# Cosmic Wishing Well · 璀璨许愿阁

> Turn a wish into a concrete next step.

This is an online cosmic wishing well. You write down what is on your mind, and it unfolds a small path from now into the future — no empty slogans, just the next step you can actually take.

## How it walks with you

**1. Make a wish** — Pick a domain and write one honest sentence. It can be "I want to build a reading habit" or "I want to finish my first short story."

**2. Get your blueprint** — A moment later you receive a personal action blueprint: an insight, a phased roadmap, habits worth cultivating, pitfalls to avoid, and a first step you can finish within 24 hours.

**3. Leave it in the forest** — If you like it, leave it in the Wish Forest. Wishes from others gather there — bless the ones that resonate, or turn yours into a poster to share.

No leaderboards. No comparisons. Every wish is taken seriously.

## In the forest

- Wander, filter and search for wishes that resonate with you
- Bless sincere wishes
- Mark your own wish as completed — a small ritual for yourself
- Switch between English and 中文 at any time
